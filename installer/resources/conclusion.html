Installed

Start your DAW and rescan its plug-ins if it does not do that by itself. Worn Vinyl appears as an effect by AE Cobley. Put it on a stereo track or bus.

Worth knowing:

  • The record turns only while your song plays. Stopped, the crackle and the wow stop too; the music still passes so you can monitor. The same passage gets the same scratches every time.
  • It delays the track by 12 ms and tells the host, which lines everything up. Wow and flutter move the timing a little either side of that — that is what wow is.
  • The older speakers are mono, as one speaker in one cabinet is; only the 1970s hi-fi plays in stereo. Speaker Off is the line out.
