Worn Vinyl

Your track played back off a worn record: the decade’s recording sound from the 1920s to the 1970s, shellac or vinyl, with scratches (the needle can stick and skip), dust, wow, flutter and warp — and the speaker it comes out of, from a hand-cranked horn gramophone to a 1970s hi-fi. A stereo effect, as a VST3 and an Audio Unit.

This installs Worn Vinyl for every user on this Mac:

  • VST3 — /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
  • Audio Unit — /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components

You choose which on the Installation Type pane. Installing over an existing copy replaces it; your projects and their settings are untouched.

Quit your DAW before installing. A host that is already running holds the old plug-in open and will not see the new one until it is restarted. Requires macOS 12 or later.
