How audio moves through the plug-in, from the stereo input to the stereo output. The music is written onto a turning “disc”, read back with the record’s faults, given the decade’s recording sound, joined by the record’s surface as the chosen player hears it, then mixed with the dry signal.

Latency

The read head rests 12 ms behind the write head, so wow, flutter and warp can bend the pitch both ways. The plug-in reports it (576 samples at 48 kHz) and the host compensates. Dry, Mix and Bypass use the same 12 ms, so nothing phases or jumps.

Jumps

Not compensated, on purpose. A stuck needle replays the last turn one to four times, then skips forward to where the song is now, so the output always returns to sync. Turn JUMPS off for no timing changes at all.

Transport

The record turns only while the song plays. On stop the disc and the screen freeze, the surface fades out in 30 ms and a stuck needle lets go; the music still passes. Every start or loop sets the disc from the song position, so a passage gets the same scratches every time.

Speed

The motor’s rpm (Auto, 33, 45 or 78) sets how often the faults come round: scratch ticks, wow, the warp, a stuck groove’s length. The music keeps its own pitch and tempo.
